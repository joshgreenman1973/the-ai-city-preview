- Quote: "The average number of days to complete first plan review through DOB NOW for all applications increased from 3.7 days in Fiscal 2025 to 5.4 days in Fiscal 2026. - `the-inventory`: The Local Law 35 dataset (jaw4-yuem) has 218 rows for 2022 to 2025 (86 in 2025); the Department of Buildings has never filed an entry.
