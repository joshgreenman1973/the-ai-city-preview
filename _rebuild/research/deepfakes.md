C6. New York Election Law 14-106(5) defines "materially deceptive media" as media that looks indistinguishable from reality to a reasonable person, depicts something that did not happen and is made with software, AI or other technical means.
- Source: N.Y. Election Law § 14-106, via New York Public Law (mirror of nysenate.gov text; "Last modified: Feb. 6, 2026", "Verified: Oct. 17, 2026") — https://newyork.public.law/laws/n.y._election_law_section_14-106 ; official page https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ELN/14-106 (WebFetch of the official page showed "Viewing most recent revision (from 2026-02-06)")
- Quote: "'Materially deceptive media' means any image, video, audio, text, or any technological representation of speech or conduct fully or partially created or modified that: (1) exhibits a high level of authenticity or convincing appearance that is visually or audibly indistinguishable from reality to a reasonable person; (2) depicts a scenario that did not actually occur or that has been altered in a significant way from how they actually occurred; and (3) is created by or with software, machine learning, artificial intelligence, or any other computer-generated or technological means"
- Last checked: Oct. 19, 2026; most recent revision Feb. 6, 2026.

C7. The law requires anyone distributing a political communication with materially deceptive media, with actual knowledge it is deceptive, to disclose it with the words "This (image, video, or audio) has been manipulated."
- Source: as C6
- Quote: "A person, firm, association, corporation, campaign, committee, or organization that distributes or publishes any political communication that was produced by or includes materially deceptive media and has actual knowledge that it is materially deceptive shall be required to disclose this use." ... "to read as follows: 'This (image, video, or audio) has been manipulated'."
- Last checked: as C6.

C9. The subdivision's remedy is a lawsuit by the candidate depicted, in state supreme court, for an injunction plus court costs and attorneys' fees; the plaintiff must prove the use of materially deceptive media by clear and convincing evidence.
- Source: as C6

"(vi) In any action commenced under this subdivision, the plaintiff bears the burden of establishing the use of materially deceptive media by clear and convincing evidence."

- Quote: "We administer five survey experiments to over 15,000 American adults detailing hypothetical politician responses to stories describing real politician scandals.
