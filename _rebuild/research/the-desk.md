## Claims

C1. The original LOADinG Act (S.7543-B) was signed Dec. 21, 2024, as Chapter 674 of 2024. It applied to "state agency," defined as state bodies, excluding the legislature and judiciary, and barred the use of automated decision-making systems from displacing state employees.
- Source: Bill S07543 (2023-24), New York State Assembly bill page (summary, actions, text) — https://nyassembly.gov/leg/?default_fld=&leg_video=&bn=S07543&term=2023&Summary=Y&Actions=Y&Text=Y
- Quote: "12/21/2024 SIGNED CHAP.674" ... "'State agency' shall mean any department, public authority, board, bureau, commission, division, office, council, committee or officer of the state. Such terms shall not include the legislature or judiciary." ... "The use of an automated decision-making system shall not result in the: (1) discharge, displacement or loss of position, including partial displacement such as a reduction in the hours of non-overtime work, wages, or employment benefits"
- Last checked for newer developments: superseded in part by C2, C4, C5.

C2. A chapter amendment (S.822), signed Feb. 14, 2025, as Chapter 96 of 2025, repealed the impact-assessment sections, narrowed the law to disclosure of automated employment decision tools, created a state AI inventory, and moved the job protections into Civil Service Law section 80, subdivision 10, which expires July 1, 2028. The underlying act took effect July 1, 2025.
- Source: Bill S00822 (2025-26), New York State Assembly bill page — https://nyassembly.gov/leg/?default_fld=&leg_video=&bn=S00822&term=2025&Summary=Y&Actions=Y&Text=Y

"section five of this act shall expire and be deemed repealed July 1, 2028." ... "§ 4. This act shall take effect [ immediately ... ] July 1, 2025 ."

- Last checked: see C4, C5.

C3. The job protections attach to "artificial intelligence systems" as defined in State Technology Law section 103-e, which covers systems that may "directly impact the public," meaning those that control or meaningfully influence outcomes affecting the public's safety, welfare or rights.
- Source: same as C2.
- Quote: "'artificial intelligence system' shall mean a machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments that, when used, may 'directly impact the public'." ...
