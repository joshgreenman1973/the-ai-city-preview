## For Josh to decide
- The essay now tells the City to make a deposit this year and restore the in-year reserves. That spends real money (the in-year reserves alone are $1 billion short of normal). The evidence supports it as an argument, but it is a stronger position than the first version took.
- "The adopted budget did not plan to spend it" (of above-forecast revenue) is a fair description of how the budget is built, but in practice the City routinely uses surplus revenue for prepayments of next year's costs; the comptroller's Proposal 5 critique mentions the prepayment. If you want to be safe, soften to "money the adopted budget was not counting on."
- The essay stops short of telling readers how to vote on Proposal 5. The comptroller calls it progress with weaknesses, and that is what the essay reports.
