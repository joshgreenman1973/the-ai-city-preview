## Judgment calls for the editor
- The essay's spine ("every/each federal proposal spares a government's own-use rules") rests on four texts. The Great American AI Act spares own-use rules only indirectly (it exempts "use or deployment" laws); Punchbowl (July 15, 2026) reported Obernolte said the state-law section could "absolutely" be revamped, and Majority Leader Scalise has called for "strong preemption." Blackburn's draft is unchecked. I softened to "the main" proposals; the editor may want a clause acknowledging the House text is still in flux.
- All the federal carve-outs say "state." The essay concedes this ("none spells out how it applies to cities") and then argues by analogy. Fair, but it is argument, not fact; the House moratorium shows Congress names "political subdivisions" when it means them.
- The Commerce list and BEAD notice apparently never appeared (at least not by April 2026). The essay's "told the Commerce Department to list" is accurate; an editor may want to note the list never came, which would modestly weaken "Colorado is where the pressure has shown" as the only visible enforcement. I did not add it.
- The essay frames the Oct. 5 hearing as about catastrophic risk; OTI's own testimony at the same hearing was largely about the algorithmic-accountability office the essay champions. The new sentence in paragraph 3 partly reflects that. The editor may want to adjust "That hearing would be shorter than the one on Oct. 5" framing, since the City's own-use office was in fact on that docket.
- Paragraph 3's added Menin quote comes from the hearing transcript (the inventory checker's saved copy), not from the press release; the linked source is OTI's written testimony. Consider linking amNewYork (https://www.amny.com/politics/nyc-ai-cyberattacks-safeguards/) as a second source.
- The nyc.gov testimony PDF returns 403 to scripted fetches; the link should be tested in a browser before publishing.
- "Even Hodan Omar" implies industry-funded groups favor preemption; Omar's quote supports a procurement carve-out, so the "even" works, but it leans on the funder line.
