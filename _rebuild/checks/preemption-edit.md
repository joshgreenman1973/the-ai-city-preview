## Headline and dek
- Headline kept: The AI Rules a City Can Keep
- Dek, before: "Each of the main federal plans to preempt state AI laws since July 2025 spares a government's rules for its own use of AI. New York City has written those rules but is still hiring for the office that enforces them."
- Dek, after: "The main federal plans to preempt state AI laws leave alone a state's rules for its own use of the technology. New York City wrote rules like that for its agencies last year and is still hiring the office that enforces them."
- Why: the old dek said the carve-outs spare "a government's" rules, which quietly extended them to cities. The new dek says "state's" and "rules like that," so the analogy is not smuggled into the first thing readers see. Chart title changed the same way.
