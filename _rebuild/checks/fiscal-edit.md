## Headline and dek
- Before: "The AI Boom Is Paying New York's Bills. Bank Some of It" / "The City's own fiscal watchdog finds that a bust in AI investment would hit revenue faster than AI taking office jobs, and the budget plans for neither"
- After: "An AI Bust Would Hit New York's Budget Faster Than AI Job Losses" / "The City's financial plan leaves out both risks, and this year's Wall Street windfall is a chance to start saving for them."

The new one carries the essay's finding, which the report supports: in fiscal 2027, AI Falls Flat leaves revenue $4.2 billion below baseline and AI Shockwave $1.1 billion (C6; C7 note, confirmed in the check report's "Better statistic" section).
