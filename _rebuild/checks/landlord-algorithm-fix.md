## Review items

### 1. DCWP "generally agreed" with recommendations including "research and other leads" (Fix these first; Problems by essay)
**Verdict: review right.** The essay took the comptroller's summary line at face value. DCWP's own response, printed at the end of the report (PDF pp. 36-39), rejects or narrows several recommendations.
- Rec. 9 (research and other leads): "DCWP rejects this recommendation to the extent it suggests DCWP must commit additional resources to identifying potential noncompliance with LL 144 through research and leads other than complaints." Its reason: "we have seen no evidence of widespread non-compliance to warrant such allocation."
- The "another" the review mentions is Rec. 7 (study why complaints are so few): "DCWP rejects this recommendation." DCWP also would not adopt Rec. 12 (investigate the auditors' 17 findings) "in full," limited Rec. 11 (annual audit monitoring) to "businesses DCWP has engaged with," and adopted Rec. 10 (OTI workbook) only "to the extent using such resources is necessary."
- The comptroller's line is still in the report (p. 25: "DCWP officials generally agreed with the report's recommendations"), so the essay now quotes it and then says what the response actually did.

**Does the argument still stand?** Yes, and it is stronger. The old essay proposed DCWP "make that a routine," as if the agency had signed on. In fact DCWP refused to spend more on looking for violations and said it saw "no evidence of widespread non-compliance," in reply to an audit that found at least 17 possible violations among 22 employers it had reviewed. The essay now argues that (a) the mayor and the Council should fund the sweep, since cost is DCWP's stated objection, and (b) the Council should amend Local Law 144 to require the sweep and a published report, since the agency has said it won't commit more resources without being made to. It also uses the comptroller's reply that DCWP acknowledged applicants cannot know when a tool was used, which is why complaints don't come. Added as dossier C38.
