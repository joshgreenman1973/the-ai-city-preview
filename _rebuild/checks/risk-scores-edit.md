The paragraph keeps the accurate status (in Assembly Codes; Senate version voted down in Senate Codes twice, most recently in May) and replaces "If lawmakers revive it, they should write in ..." with "Any state tool should come with what New York City already provides: ..." 4. Eight vs. seven factors. Dropped the count. The sentence now reads "Its factors cover bench warrants, convictions, pending cases, how long the person has lived at an address and whether the person can be reached by phone," which is true under either count.
5. "The most recent published numbers cover 2021 to 2023." Kept; the checker found nothing newer, and it is now the closing sentence. If Josh wants extra safety: "The most recent published study covers 2021 to 2023."
6. Kleinberg parenthetical. Took the checker's suggestion: "failures to appear in court" throughout, parenthetical about the paper's use of "crime" dropped. C46 supports this reading. Also changed "the economists Jon Kleinberg, Jens Ludwig, Sendhil Mullainathan and colleagues" to drop "economists"; the dossier does not describe the authors' fields, and Kleinberg is a computer scientist.
