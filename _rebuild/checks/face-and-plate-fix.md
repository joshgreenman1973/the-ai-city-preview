## The argument
The essay is stronger now. It says the City should not renew ShotSpotter when the contract ends in December 2027 unless the next contract measures confirmed shootings and the NYPD publishes that count every month. Support: the auditors' 8% to 20% confirmation rate; their recommendation against renewing without a fuller evaluation, which the NYPD rejected before renewing anyway; and the vendor's 2025 announcement, which still described the 90% gunfire-location standard. The opposing case comes where it matters, after the thesis: ShotSpotter catches shootings nobody calls in (33 of 122), the NYPD says later evidence can confirm more alerts, and the department says no one has offered it a better measure. The essay answers that the Council should write the measure. It does not argue for dropping ShotSpotter outright; the dossier does not support that.
