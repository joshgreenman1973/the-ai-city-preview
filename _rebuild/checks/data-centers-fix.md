## Review items

### 1. "Chart groups zones F-I as 'downstate.' NYISO counts F as upstate; number unaffected." (Problems by essay)
Verdict: review wrong on the definition, right that the number is unaffected.

NYISO's own definition in Power Trends 2026 puts Zone F with downstate. The installed-capacity and energy charts are labeled: "Upstate Summer Installed Capacity, 2026 (Zones A-E)" / "Downstate Summer Installed Capacity, 2026 (Zones F-K)" / "Upstate Energy, 2025 (Zones A-E)" / "Downstate Energy, 2025 (Zones F-K)." Source: https://www.nyiso.com/documents/d/guest/2026-power-trends (local copy `_rebuild/checks/pdfs/data-centers/powertrends.pdf`). Nothing in the 2026 Gold Book text groups Zone F with upstate.

The 2040 Zone F figure is zero. Gold Book Table I-14, "Summer Peak Demand by Zone - MW," p. 58 (PDF page 62), read from the rendered page. The 2040 row, Zones A through K and then NYCA: "2040 440 411 1,050 435 65 0 292 0 0 0 187 2,880." Zone F is 0 in every year 2026 through 2041. All 292 MW in F-I is in Zone G.

What changed anyway: the old row label "Other downstate zones (F-I)" was accurate under NYISO's definition, but it had the Albany region sitting under "downstate," which reads wrong to most people and drew the review's objection. The chart now shows Zone G on its own and F, H and I as a zero row:

| Zone | MW in 2040 |
| Upstate (Zones A-E) | 2,401 |
| Hudson Valley (Zone G) | 292 |
| Long Island (Zone K) | 187 |
| Capital region and other Hudson Valley zones (F, H, I) | 0 |
| New York City (Zone J) | 0 |
| Statewide total | 2,880 |

2,401 + 292 + 187 + 0 + 0 = 2,880. The source line now states the definition: upstate means Zones A-E and downstate Zones F-K, as NYISO defines them in Power Trends 2026. The chart title went from "Almost all" to "Most," since 2,401 of 2,880 is 83%. The chart dek no longer uses the acronym and does not repeat the essay dek. New dossier claim C32 records both quotes.
