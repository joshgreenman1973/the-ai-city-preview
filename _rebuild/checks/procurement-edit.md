## Headline and dek
- Before: "Put the AI Rules in the Contract" / "New York City has passed laws to oversee its algorithms, but what a vendor owes the City is still negotiated one deal at a time"
- After: "Make the City's AI Contract Terms Public and Mandatory" / "New York City's technology office has written standard AI terms for software contracts. They should be published and required, with added clauses on accuracy, false alarms and the City's right to walk away."
- Why: the old dek implied the City had no standard terms. OTI's Oct. 5 testimony (C30) says it does. The thesis now asks the City to publish the rider, add performance testing, error reporting and exit terms, and require it.
