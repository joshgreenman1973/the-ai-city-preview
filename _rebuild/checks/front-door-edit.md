One broader negative remains in the counter-case paragraph ("the City never published an accuracy rate for it"); it rests on the same check and Josh may want it narrowed too.

The text now states the facts (the city trimmed 311's hours and staff to close a budget gap and pointed callers to Sunny), says "The city presented the cut as a budget measure," and assigns the inference to a skeptic: "A skeptic would say the bot made it easier to make."

The difference is in the answers": the youth department's bot returns the best match from a staff-maintained knowledge base (C15, C28); MyCity generated replies with GPT-4o (C7). The paragraph's opener no longer claims a different "design."
5. **Bot once answered the voucher question correctly.** Added to the lede: "At one point it told a reporter they did. Then 10 Markup staff members asked the same question, and the bot told every one of them no." The inconsistency supports the argument for measured accuracy.

Also from the check report: kept the GOV.UK self-grading caveat and "rose from 76 percent to 90 percent" as is. Did not add the total-contacts figure (tried it, cut it for length).
