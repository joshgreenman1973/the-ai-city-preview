## For Josh

- The headline says the laws "stop at the data vendor's door." That is a fair reading of the identifying information law (vendors outside human services are covered only if the chief privacy officer designates them) and of the NYPD's crime-investigation exemption, but the resource ban does name "contractors," and the NYPD's own policy bars immigration sharing. If that feels too strong, the old headline is the safe fallback.
- Still not confirmed, as in round one: whether the government appealed the Oct. 15 dismissal, whether EO 13's May 7 reports were published, and whether the chief privacy officer has ever designated a non-human-services vendor. The essay claims none of these.
- The recommendation to cut the NYPD's retention is an argument. The cold-case rationale is stated as "the usual argument," not attributed to the NYPD, because no source in the dossier quotes the department on it.
